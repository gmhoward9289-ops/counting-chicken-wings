### The README said the corpus was three subjects; it is six

`## Scope` still claimed "three subjects are live — broiler wings, table eggs,
and saffron", written before ground beef, maple and silk landed. It now names
all six, says where vanilla actually stands (a species with a curing loss and no
product yet), and the yield-mode table carries an example from each of the three
modes rather than from the original three subjects. The mixing paragraph swapped
its milk analogy — which is not in the corpus — for ground beef, which is.

The landing section now says on the first screen that the wing is the flagship
question rather than the whole corpus, so the wider scope is visible without
scrolling to `## Scope`. A stray `+` from a botched patch, which had been
rendering under the title on the repo page, is gone.
