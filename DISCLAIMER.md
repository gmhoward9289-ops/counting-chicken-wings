## What this site is

This site presents a statistical model that estimates figures like "how many chickens
does it take to make a dozen wings." The underlying numbers come from public
agricultural data sources (USDA, FAO, and similar), but the specific figures shown
here — ratios, estimates, derived calculations — are this project's own
interpretation and modeling of that data, not official government or industry
statistics.
