[18]
  slug: chilling-method-yield
  title:
    Moisture retention by water- and air-chilled broilers, and effect of
    immersion or dry air chilling on carcass moisture retention
  publisher: Poultry Science
  source_type: peer_reviewed
  retrieved_on: 2026-07-28
  url: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S105661711931205X
  notes:
    Air chilling LOSES 2.5-2.84% of prechill weight; immersion chilling GAINS
    4.14% in one study and 9.3% in another, with a range of 3.4-14.7%. Overall
    carcass yield: immersion +6.5%, combination inline air +1.98%, air chill
    -1.10%. This is a MASS effect only. Chilling method changes what a wing
    weighs by up to a tenth; it cannot change how many wings a chicken has. It
    therefore belongs in the mass stages and can never move a count answer -
    but it matters a great deal to anyone buying wings by the pound.
